# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Does this system require an active internet connection to work?
**No.** The core POS billing, inventory management, customer dues tracking, and UPI QR code generation work **100% offline**. Data is saved locally inside your browser/PC database engine. Internet is only required if you use optional Google Gemini AI stock reordering insights.

---

### Q2: Does dynamic UPI payment require an active payment gateway subscription?
**No.** The dynamic UPI module constructs standard compliant UPI payment URIs (`upi://pay?pa=...`) rendered into high-resolution QR codes directly on the screen. Funds transfer directly into your linked shop bank account without gateway commissions or third-party fees.

---

### Q3: How do I back up my shop inventory and sales data?
Navigate to **Backup & Restore** in the left sidebar menu. Click **Export Complete Database (.json)** to download a full backup copy of your products, sales history, customer ledgers, and settings.

---

### Q4: Can I run this POS on a thermal receipt printer?
**Yes.** Use standard thermal printers (e.g., 80mm or 58mm). Click **Print Receipt** after completing a sale or set browser auto-print settings.

---

### Q5: How do I lock the terminal when stepping away?
Click the **Store Owner / Lock** icon in the top header or press the lock button. Access requires entering your 4-digit Security PIN.
